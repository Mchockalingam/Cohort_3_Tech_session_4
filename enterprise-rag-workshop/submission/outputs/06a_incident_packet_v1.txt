Section: Payments API P1 Incident Runbook > Trigger and severity
## Trigger and severity

Declare a P1 when the Payments API has more than **5% HTTP 5xx responses for five consecutive minutes** and customer checkout is materially affected. Start the incident bridge and assign an incident commander. Do not treat a single failed request as a P1.

Section: Database Connection Pool Saturation Runbook > Detect and verify
## Detect and verify

On `payments-api`, inspect pool active/idle/pending connections, database max connections, queue wait time and timeouts. Treat **greater than 90% utilization for five minutes** with increasing timeouts as evidence of pool saturation, not definitive root cause.

Section: Emergency Production Change Policy > Approval and evidence
## Approval and evidence
An emergency rollback requires approval from **the incident commander and an independent on-call change approver** before execution. The change record must identify the incident, affected service, proposed rollback version, risk assessment, verification metrics, executor and approvers. The executor cannot act as the independent approver.

Section: Incident Severity and Communication Policy > Operating targets
## Operating targets

P1 on-call acknowledgement target: **10 minutes**. The incident commander owns coordination. Provide an approved customer-facing update at least **every 30 minutes** while the P1 remains open. Updates must not include credentials or personally identifiable information.

Section: Database Connection Pool Saturation Runbook > After mitigation
## After mitigation

Verify pool utilization falls below **75%** and payment error rate below **1%** for at least **15 minutes**. Record the change ID, evidence and observed effect. Use a staged rollback if one is approved and supported by the release procedure.
